SRI KALISWARI COLLEGE

ABOUT ADMISSION DETAILS






As one of the few NAAC accredited (A+ Grade) institutions in the region, Sri Kaliswari College is poised to become one among the premiere educational institutions in the country. Thanks to the unceasing efforts of the trustees of Sri Kaliswari Trust, and the faculty, the college as a whole, is dedicated to raising the bar of infrastructure and elevating holistic education to international standards. Incepted in the year 2000 in Sivakasi, the college was expanded and moved to a larger campus in A- Meenakshipuram, seven kilmetres from Sivakasi, by 19th March, 2001. Recognised by the UGC, Sri Kaliswari College became autonomous in 2012.
